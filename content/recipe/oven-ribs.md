## Meta
- Serves 2 per rack of ribs
- TOTAL TIME 3h to 4h

## Ingredients
- Pork Ribs/Baby back ribs (any), N racks
- Mustard, enough to cover all racks of ribs
- Any BBQ rub mix, enough to cover all racks of ribs
- Any BBQ sauce, enough to cover all racks of ribs

## Equipment
- Required
	- Oven, preferably with broil function
	- Aluminium foil
- Optional
	- Brush to apply BBQ Sauce
	- Aluminium tray / Baking tray to catch drip
	- Meat thermometer

## Instructions
1. *If frozen*, thaw ribs at room temp
2. Use knife to trim excess fat/meat
3. Use knife and force to remove entire membrane
4. Brush entire rib rack surface with mustard, even coat, not too much (u cant taste anyways)
5. Apply BBQ rub to entire rib rack surface, generously
6. Wrap rib in 2-3 layers of alum. foil, cover all sides tightly
7. If applicable, place wrapped in alum. tray or baking tray
8. Place in middle rack of 140C (285F) oven, *TIMER* 2.5h to *TIMER* 3.5h, the longer the better
9. Take ribs out of foil, expose upper surface. Optional: **CHECK EVERY 30m**
10. Brush ribs with layer of BBQ Sauce
11. OPTION **If your oven has BROIL mode**, set broil to HIGH, place in TOP rack and broil for *TIMER* 2m to *TIMER* 5m, **CHECK EVERY 1m** to prevent burning sauce, take out after sauce bubbles slightly.
12. OPTION **If your oven does not have BROIL mode but you have a propane torch**, torch the upper surface until bubbling
13. OPTION **If your oven does not have BROIL mode and you have no torch**, SET oven to 220C (430F) and place in TOP rack for *TIMER* 3m to *TIMER* 10m, **CHECK EVERY 1m** to prevent burning sauce, take out after sauce bubbles slightly.
